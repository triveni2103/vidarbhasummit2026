About the Summit

Where Skills Meet Intelligence

The Vidarbha Skill & AI Summit 2026 is a landmark regional convening that brings together educators, policymakers, students, and innovators to explore the intersection of vocational skill education and artificial intelligence in schools.

Hosted by Lend A Hand India, celebrating 20 years of Skill Education in Schools, this summit creates a platform for dialogue, demonstration, and discovery — anchored in the lived reality of Vidarbha's schools and communities.

How Skill Education and AI are shaping the future of School Education in Vidarbha

Join us for an afternoon of student showcases, live demonstrations, expert panels, and a skit that brings NEP 2020 to life — followed by a shared lunch celebrating the spirit of skilled India.
